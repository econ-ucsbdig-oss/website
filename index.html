    Investment Policy

    Summary of the fund's Investment Policy Statement.

    Read the full statement

    Strategy
    Long-only U.S. equity, benchmark-aware ("enhanced index"). Returns come from bottom-up stock selection, disciplined position sizing, and research-supported tilts.
    Benchmark
    S&P 500 ex-Energy Index, with a target tracking error of about 4–6% a year.
    Portfolio
    Up to 30 positions, mostly individual stocks. Positions usually start at 2–3% and are capped at 10%. Sector weights stay within about five points of the benchmark.
    Investments
    U.S.-listed stocks, ADRs, and REITs, plus cash. No derivatives, leverage, short sales, or private securities. Tobacco and fossil-fuel companies are excluded.
    Decisions
    Every trade requires a majority vote of members and approval from the faculty advisors. Portfolio and risk reviews happen each quarter.
